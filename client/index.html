About Velocity AI

Velocity AI is an intelligent academic learning platform engineered to help students bridge the gap between note-taking and deep concept mastery. Traditional studying methods—such as rereading textbooks, highlighting paragraphs, and reviewing static summaries—create an illusion of competence. In contrast, cognitive psychology demonstrates that active retrieval practice and spaced repetition produce superior retention and exam performance.

By combining high-speed document extraction (PyMuPDF and optical character recognition) with Google Gemini AI models, Velocity AI automatically turns dense educational documents into structured study chapters and interactive testing quizzes. Whether preparing for high school finals, university midterms, medical licensing tests, or technical certifications, Velocity AI reduces preparation hours while maximizing retention.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Velocity AI features, pricing, and study workflows.

What file formats does Velocity AI support?

Velocity AI supports PDF documents, Microsoft PowerPoint presentations (.pptx), Word documents (.docx), plain text files (.txt), images (PNG, JPG) with text OCR, and audio lecture recordings. Read our Getting Started Guide for optimal scanning tips.

How does the AI quiz generator create questions?

Our AI examines the core terminology, definitions, formulas, and concepts extracted from your uploaded files and automatically creates multiple-choice practice quizzes. Each question includes four plausible answer choices and a detailed explanation of why the correct answer is right. Learn more in our Interactive Tutorials.

Is Velocity AI free to use?

Yes, Velocity AI offers a 100% Free Tier that includes 5 document uploads per day, basic text summarization, and 3 practice quizzes every day. Students needing higher volume can upgrade anytime on our Plans & Pricing page.

How does the Adaptive Study Planner calculate my schedule?

You enter your exam date and subjects. The study planner calculates the days remaining, evaluates your daily target study minutes, breaks your curriculum down into focused study blocks, and dynamically adjusts whenever you complete or reschedule sessions.
